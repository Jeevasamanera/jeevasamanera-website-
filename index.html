About

ARTISTIC / CURATORIAL CONCEPT OF MY ART

Jeeva Samanera is an artist, philosopher, creator exploring ideas of consciousness, spirituality, human identity, symbolism and transformation through visual art.

I am using charcoal as a medium and a metaphor in order to explore the fragile space between human vulnerability and transformation, between light and its shadow, between presence and disappearance, between positive and negative space.

The monochromatic language of charcoal allows me to strip away distractions and reveal essential emotional and psychological multidimensional landscapes, investigating the emotional and symbolic dimensions of human consciousness and spirituality.

In my recent works, this inquiry has evolved into a more focused examination of traces. As an artist and curator, I am interested in encounters that invite contemplation rather than certainty.

My practice seeks to transcend fleeting spiritual experiences into lasting essence of humanity through structural visual experiences of sequential philosophical insights, contrast of sequential patterns and tonal depth, encouraging viewers to reflect on their own stories of loss, resilience, belonging and renewal through portraiture, symbolism, atmospheric imagery with kairolume — a moment when purpose and clarity converge.

Introduction

REFLECTRE is an ongoing exploration of consciousness, perception and the relationship between the inner and outer worlds. Through charcoal, portraiture, symbolism and atmospheric imagery, the series investigates memory, vulnerability, transformation and spiritual experience.

Each work exists as a fragment of a larger philosophical landscape. Rather than offering definitive answers, REFLECTRE creates spaces for contemplation — inviting the viewer to encounter their own memories, emotions and perceptions within the work.
