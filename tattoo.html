ARTISTIC CONCEPT

I approach tattooing as a form of visual reflection — a meeting point between the client's personal story and my own artistic language. I am interested in emotion, symbolism, contrast and the transformation of an idea into something permanently carried on the body.

SELECTED WORK

THE PRACTICE

Tattooing since 2021, with a private studio practice in Lisbon, Portugal. My work moves between realism, blackwork, abstract composition and symbolic imagery, with an emphasis on creating pieces that carry a personal and emotional meaning for the wearer.
