INTRODUCTION

REFLECTRE

REFLECTRE is an ongoing exploration of consciousness, perception and the relationship between the inner and outer worlds. Through charcoal, portraiture, symbolism and atmospheric imagery, the series investigates memory, vulnerability, transformation and spiritual experience.

ARTWORK 01

THE SPACE

What happens out there, happens somewhere prior in a consciousness. The Space is a reflective field of awareness which reflects the human consciousness. This work explores the possibility that what we perceive as the physical universe may also be understood as a projection of consciousness.
