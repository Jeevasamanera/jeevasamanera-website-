INTRODUCTION

REFLECTRE

REFLECTRE is an ongoing exploration of consciousness, perception, and the relationship between the inner and outer worlds.

Through charcoal, portraiture, symbolism, and atmospheric imagery, the series investigates memory, vulnerability, transformation, and spiritual experience.

Each work emerges from an attempt to observe the invisible structures that shape how we experience ourselves and the world around us.

Each work therefore invites the viewer to pause, observe, and recognise something of their own experience within the image.

02

REFLECTRE does not seek to define consciousness or offer a singular interpretation of existence. Instead, it creates a space for contemplation — a place where perception can become uncertain, where memory can overlap with imagination, and where the boundary between the internal and external begins to dissolve.

The works are fragments of a larger philosophical landscape. They explore the possibility that what we perceive is never entirely separate from the consciousness through which we perceive it.

In this sense, reflection becomes more than the act of looking at an image. It becomes an encounter with oneself.

REFLECTRE is not a conclusion.
It is an invitation to look again.
